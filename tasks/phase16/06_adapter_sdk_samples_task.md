# Task 06: Adapter SDK Samples

## Why

Docs mention future Go backend samples and Lua-like automation hooks. These could help external teams map their systems into roomci, but they could also distract from the core emulator.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Decide whether to ship:
  - a Go backend sample client,
  - TypeScript sample client,
  - Lua-like automation hook examples,
  - or docs-only pseudocode.
- Keep samples focused on adapter contracts and local/CI evaluation.
- Do not introduce a second runtime unless it clearly improves evaluator adoption.
