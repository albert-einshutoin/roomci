# Task 02: Network And Control-Panel Fault Profiles

## Why

Network segmentation and control-panel faults are central to hospitality smart-home QA and improve broader building-automation relevance.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Add deterministic scenario profiles for VLAN/segment isolation, firewall drift, local broker unreachable, UPS degraded, circuit-protector trip, redundant PSU fault, and edge-computer failover evidence where practical.
- Model them as QA fault profiles with report/BMS evidence, not vendor or electrical simulators.
- Add passing and failing examples plus runner tests.
- Update hospitality coverage and evaluator checklist docs.

## Out of Scope

- Physical electrical safety validation.
- UniFi/YAMAHA/industrial switch emulation.
