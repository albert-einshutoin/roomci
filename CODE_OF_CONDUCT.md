# Code of Conduct

We want roomci discussions and contributions to be welcoming and constructive.

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions.
- Welcome people of all backgrounds and perspectives.
- Criticize ideas and behavior, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, or disclose another person's private information.

Maintainers may moderate public issues and pull requests that violate these
expectations. A confidential conduct-reporting contact and response owner have
not yet been designated. Do not put personal details or private evidence in a
public issue. You may ask for a private contact in a public issue without
describing the incident; a maintainer must confirm a suitable channel before
you share details.
